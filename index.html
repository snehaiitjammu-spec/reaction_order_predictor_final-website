Method-selection logic used by the website

Integrated method

n=0: C=C0−kt
n=1: ln C=ln C0−kt
n≠1: C1−n=C01−n+(n−1)kt

Strong choice when the complete concentration–time curve is reasonably smooth, especially when numerical differentiation would magnify noise.

Differential method

−dC/dt=kCn
ln(rate)=ln k+n ln C

Strong choice when there are enough well-spaced, low-noise data points to estimate rates reliably. It is much more sensitive to noise because derivatives are computed from differences.

Educational tool. Experimental noise, changing temperature, changing volume, side reactions, and concentration measurement errors can affect the inferred order. An excellent fit does not by itself prove a reaction mechanism.
