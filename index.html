Method background

Integrated method

0th: C=C₀−kt
1st: ln C=ln C₀−kt
n≠1: C1−n=C₀1−n+(n−1)kt

Uses the complete concentration–time history and is usually less sensitive to noise than numerical differentiation.

Differential method

−dC/dt=kCn
ln(rate)=ln k+n ln C

Estimates rates directly. It is a useful independent test when the data are sufficiently numerous, smooth, and well spaced.
